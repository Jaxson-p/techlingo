📘 Options quick reference

Put

Gives the buyer the right to sell at the contract's strike price under its terms.

Call

Gives the buyer the right to buy at the contract's strike price under its terms.

Premium

The price paid to buy an option contract.

Expiration

The contract's end date. Time remaining affects an option's value.
